## If asked
- **"How many users will you have?"** We don't claim a number. Our first users are university students in Azerbaijan, reached through mentors' and space agencies' school-lesson programs; Backyard Worlds shows volunteers will blink sky images in large numbers (F9).
- **"What does it cost to run?"** The showcase is static files on free hosting; the only running cost is one small server for new sky positions (today a trial tier). Each new field is cached after the first request.
- **"Can it scale to the whole sky?"** Yes — fields are built on demand by position, with no hard-coded file lists; SPHEREx data for new positions is the slow part (precomputed only for the 5 NASA targets today).
- **Limits to say openly:** live builds are WISE-only today (SPHEREx frames are precomputed for the NASA targets); the 12 s was one measurement on one position.
