## If asked
- **"How did you measure half a minute?"** Two independent tests on new, uncached positions: WISE frames ready in 11–23 s, finished in 20–33 s (up to 38 s seen) — `science/IMPACT_NUMBERS.md` §1.
- **"Where do 'hours' come from?"** It is an estimate, stated as such: 46–96 WISE images + ~45 min of SPHEREx downloads (measured times per request) + at least 1 h of cleaning/aligning + 30–60 min blinking and measuring ≈ 2–3 h per field for an expert (`science/IMPACT_NUMBERS.md` §4).
- **"How many users will you have?"** We don't claim a number. First users: university students and the SRDA community; Backyard Worlds shows volunteers will blink sky images in large numbers.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** Website on a free tier, API on trial credits — budget zero; the five NASA targets are static files with no server.
- **Limits to say openly:** live builds are WISE-only today; SPHEREx frames are precomputed for the five NASA targets.
