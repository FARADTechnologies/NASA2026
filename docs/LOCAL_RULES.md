# LOCAL RULES — Baku 2026 (filled by the pitch role from Amal's notes of the Saturday rules session, Sat 14:10)

## Time
- **Presentation + DEMO together: 5 minutes total.** We decide the split inside those 5 minutes.
- **Q&A: 5 minutes.** Total slot: 10 minutes per team.
- **Going over the limit = disqualified.** → Rehearse to ~4:40 max, with a hard cut plan.
- Order of teams: unknown (ask at the venue).

## Slides
- **Maximum 7 slides. All in English.**
- Slides must be **shared by link** (Google Drive, GitHub or OneDrive).
- Space Apps requirement: slides must show **team name, member names, and the chosen challenge**.
- Readable font, little text per slide.
- Recommended (not mandatory) structure from the organizers (based on Space Apps guidance):
  1. Title — project name + tagline, team name, member names, challenge
  2. Challenge — what problem, who it affects, why it matters
  3. Solution — the idea in 1–2 sentences: what it does, for whom
  4. How it works — picture, prototype or screenshots, user experience
  5. Space data — which NASA and partner-agency data, which tools, how used
  6. Impact — who benefits, at what scale, what changes
  7. Next steps — next phase and development plan

## AI use
- AI use is **allowed but must be shown/disclosed** (slide + project page `pitch/AI_USE.md`).
- AI-generated photos/videos are allowed **only with a watermark** (e.g. "AI-generated").
- **Never** use AI to generate the NASA logo, flags, or mission symbols/patches.
